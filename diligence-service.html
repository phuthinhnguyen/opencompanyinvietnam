Due Diligenge in Vietnam for foreign investors

We are able to support the following services:

1. Counterparty Due Diligence

We can conduct open-source and registry-based verification, including:

  • Company registration status
  • Licensing and operational status
  • Corporate information verification
  • Sanctions and watchlist screening
  • Shareholder and management review
  • Public compliance and reputational checks

Deliverable: A written due diligence report based on the requested scope and risk indicators.

2. Counterparty Reliability Check

We can assist with a deeper assessment of the target company’s reliability and operational capability, including:

  • Business history and market presence
  • Litigation and dispute checks
  • Bankruptcy and insolvency review
  • Financial and corporate background review
  • Verification through publicly available records and government-related sources where accessible

Deliverable: A comprehensive written report with supporting findings and available documentation.

3. Comprehensive Analysis / Tax Support

We also have experience handling enhanced due diligence and on-site verification assignments, including:

  • Review of corporate and operational documentation
  • Analysis of accounting and tax-related materials provided by the counterparty
  • Site visits and physical verification
  • Photography and field observations
  • Identification of operational, legal, reputational, or compliance risks
  • Checking for the banking status in Vietnam National Bank of Director and shareholders in the company whether they are in blacklisted for money-laundering/fraud.

Deliverable: A detailed assessment report and professional opinion regarding the counterparty and associated risks.

The above quotation includes field investigation and site verification in Ho Chi Minh City. Others cites of Vietnam will have different fee (+plus the transport fee)

Please note that the final scope and timeline 7-10 days may be adjusted depending on:

  • Availability of public records and documentation
  • Responsiveness of the target company
  • Additional requirements requested by the client
  • Complexity of the investigation

Note: In case the client wants to carry out the due dil without the involvement of the counterparty, we can do these kinds of service:

  • Verification of the company’s actual business operations, including confirmation of whether the company is operating at its registered address
  • Review of company registration information, including:
    • legal status
    • business lines
    • charter capital
    • history of corporate changes
    • information on the legal representative and shareholders/members
  • Verification of the company’s tax operational status with the Vietnamese tax authority system
  • Review of publicly available information relating to the company through official databases and public sources
  • Analysis and assessment of the company’s business performance, including:
    • revenue and business results
    • basic financial indicators
    • preliminary assessment of financial capability and business continuity
  • Review of publicly available information relating to:
    • litigation
    • disputes
    • enforcement actions
    • administrative sanctions (if any)
  • Screening for information related to:
    • anti-money laundering
    • blacklists
    • enforcement actions
    • sanctions lists concerning the company, its legal representative, and shareholders/members
  • Review of information relating to the company’s credit status, outstanding liabilities, and financial obligations through legally accessible sources
  • Issuance of a due diligence report, including findings and an overall assessment of the company’s risk level.

Deliverable: A detailed assessment report and professional opinion regarding the counterparty and associated risks.

The above quotation includes field investigation and site verification in Ho Chi Minh City, others cities will need to plus the transport fee.
